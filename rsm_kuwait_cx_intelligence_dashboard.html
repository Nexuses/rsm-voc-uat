Understanding the rating scales

CSAT scale — 1 to 5

Answers "Quality of services provided"

😄 5 Very satisfied — exceptional delivery, exceeds expectations
😊 4 Satisfied — quality met, professional service delivered
😐 3 Somewhat satisfied — acceptable but room to improve
😞 1–2 Dissatisfied — service fell below expectations

Scores 4 & 5 = positive. CSAT = (4s + 5s) ÷ total × 100

NPS scale — 1 to 5

Answers "Would you recommend RSM Kuwait?"

😄 5 Promoter — actively recommends RSM to others
😐 4 Neutral — satisfied but not an active advocate
😞 1–3 Detractor — unlikely to recommend; may spread negative sentiment

NPS = % promoters − % detractors. A score ≥50% is excellent for professional services

CES scale — 1 to 5

Answers "Helpful, courteous, active communicator?"

😄 5 Very easy — effortless, proactive, no friction
😊 4 Easy — smooth collaboration, responsive team
😐 3 Neutral — some effort required from client
😞 1–2 Difficult — high client effort, barriers to engagement

CES = sum of all scores ÷ number of responses. 951 ÷ 205 = 4.6

Strategic insights

CSAT: Service quality is consistently strong

97% of clients rated service at 4 or 5. Only 1 dissatisfied response across the year. The focus is to sustain this through consistent execution, clear communication, and the use of technology — particularly during peak audit periods.

NPS: Good advocacy base — growth opportunity to 70% target

NPS of 62% reflects 137 promoters and only 10 detractors. The large neutral group (58 clients) is the primary lever. Converting even 25% of neutrals to promoters would take NPS above the 70% FY2026 goal. Clearer expectation-setting and partner-led check-ins are the key interventions.

CES: Ease of engagement exceeds target — sustain through peaks

A 4.6/5 CES confirms clients find RSM easy to work with. 72% of respondents rated the experience as "very easy". The 5 clients who rated difficulty (1–2) warrant follow-up. The focus is maintaining this standard during high-volume periods through status visibility and proactive communication.

Where RSM will improve

Advocacy (NPS)

Clearer expectation-setting during project initiation and regular engagement partner check-ins to convert the 58 neutral clients into active promoters.

Visibility

Client-facing status dashboards and clearer next-action summaries to increase transparency and reduce the effort clients need to track engagement progress.

Consistency

Champion-led coaching to promote consistent delivery practices across service line teams, ensuring all clients receive the same high standard of engagement.
