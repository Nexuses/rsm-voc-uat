# Few-shot tone excerpts (Client Experience Report style)

## Excerpt A — opening philosophy

At RSM in Kuwait, our philosophy is built on the power of being understood. Central to this is a genuine understanding of the client experience. Each year, we ask three questions of ourselves: Are our clients satisfied? Is it easy to work with us? Do we continue to earn their trust?

## Excerpt B — metric outlook

A CSAT of 97% reflects steady service quality across the period. The result is drawn from responses rating the experience as satisfied or very satisfied. In line with ongoing efforts, we remain focused on sustaining this level through consistent delivery and clear communication.

An NPS of 70 indicates a base of client advocates. This reflects trust built through consistent service across engagements. We continue to focus on supporting satisfied clients to become active advocates through closer engagement and clear communication.

A CES toward the easier end of the scale suggests clients generally find it easy to work with us. This points to clear communication and responsive client-facing teams. We aim to sustain this during peak periods through greater visibility of engagement status.

## Excerpt C — closing

The results reflect our continued commitment to delivering a quality client experience. Building on results across the three measures, we plan to address areas for improvement and continue to earn the trust our clients place in us.
